## Design Philosophy

### Why This Palette?
1. **Premium Tech Aesthetic**: Purple/violet is associated with luxury, creativity, and innovation
2. **Modern SaaS Feel**: Similar to Stripe, Linear, and other premium tech products
3. **Better Contrast**: Purple provides better visual hierarchy than blue
4. **Unique Identity**: Stands out from typical blue tech portfolios
5. **Sophisticated Gradients**: Purple-to-cyan creates depth and dimension

### Color Psychology
- **Purple**: Creativity, innovation, luxury, wisdom
- **Cyan**: Technology, clarity, freshness
- **Pink**: Energy, passion, modernity
- **Deep Black**: Sophistication, elegance, focus
